Research

We develop theoretical and computational methods for novel quantum spectroscopy and dynamics. Our work brings together quantum light-matter and machine learning to explore light induced processes in molecules and nanoscale materials.

Quantum Spectroscopy and Dynamics at the Nanoscale

We are interested in light-induced quantum processes in molecules and nanoscale materials. These include the generation and transfer of quantum coherence and entanglement, their influence on charge and energy transfer, and their role in spectroscopy. We explore how confined photonic environments and the quantum nature of light can provide new ways to probe, understand, and control these processes.

Machine Learning for Quantum Light-Matter

We develop machine learning models to explore quantum light-matter interactions in molecules and nanoscale materials. Our research explores the space of material structures, photonic environments, and quantum properties of light to understand their influence on dynamics and optical responses. We use ML models to uncover novel physical mechanisms and identify the essential features governing light-matter interactions. Our goal is to guide the discovery and design of materials with tailored optical properties and functionalities for photonic and quantum technologies.
